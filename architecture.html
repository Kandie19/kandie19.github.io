What Happens at Each Layer

PERCEPTION

Everything becomes a signal

Live video, sensor telemetry and operational events enter through controlled ingestion paths. Each signal carries enough context to remain traceable to its source.

  • Camera and sensor provenance
  • Real-time stream handling
  • Event normalization
INTELLIGENCE

Signals become context

AI models and analytical services interpret what is happening: objects, identities, behaviors, anomalies and relationships between events.

  • Computer vision
  • Identity correlation
  • Behavior analysis
THREAT

Context becomes risk

Correlated evidence is assessed through risk and threat intelligence services. Confidence, severity, history and context influence the assessment.

  • Risk scoring
  • Threat prediction
  • Incident linkage
DECISION

Risk becomes a recommendation

The decision layer determines what should happen next while respecting policy, role, confidence and operational constraints.

  • Decision intelligence
  • Recommendations
  • Human-in-the-loop controls
AUTONOMY

Recommendations become action

SOAR-style playbooks execute controlled response workflows with execution state, escalation and auditability.

  • Playbook selection
  • Execution tracking
  • Escalation
OBSERVABILITY

Every operation remains visible

Metrics, event timelines, audit records and system health provide the feedback required to operate the platform safely at scale.

  • Metrics and health
  • Audit logging
  • Operational feedback

DATA

Persistence & Repositories

SQLAlchemy 2.0 models, repository abstractions, tenant-aware relationships, indexes, audit fields, soft-delete and archive semantics provide durable boundaries. PostgreSQL and vector infrastructure are the natural scale-out path.

REAL-TIME

Events & Streaming

WebSockets, Redis listeners, event buses, metrics broadcasters and detection loops allow the command center to operate on current state rather than delayed batch reports.

RELIABILITY

Operational Loops

Health monitoring, escalation scheduling, event processing, AI pipelines and response execution are treated as continuously running system capabilities.

IP BOUNDARY

What This Page Does Not Reveal

Private implementation code, proprietary model weights, private prompts, internal credentials, deployment secrets, private data and product-specific detection logic remain outside the public architecture view.

Architectural Principle

Loose coupling. Explicit contracts. Multi-tenancy. Observable operations. Secure defaults. Replaceable intelligence engines. Versioned data models. Controlled autonomy.
