SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE / 03

Designing systems that remain coherent under complexity.

Architecture is treated as the primary engineering discipline: boundaries, contracts, event flows, persistence, security, observability and failure recovery are designed together.

01 / SYSTEMS

Distributed Architecture

Service-oriented and event-driven patterns connect ingestion, analytics, identity, incidents, alerts, decisions and response without forcing every capability into one monolith.

02 / DATA

Data & Persistence

SQLAlchemy models, repositories, tenant-aware relationships, migrations and audit fields establish durable data boundaries, with PostgreSQL and vector infrastructure planned for scale.

03 / REAL-TIME

Streaming & Events

WebSockets, camera streams, event buses, Redis listeners, metrics broadcasters and detection loops support operational systems where stale information is unacceptable.

04 / SECURITY

Identity & Trust

JWT authorization, tenant context, RBAC, audit logging, service boundaries and soft-delete/archive semantics are embedded into the platform architecture.
