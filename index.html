ARCHITECT'S VIEW

FROM SIGNAL TO OUTCOME

The important architectural decision is not simply detecting an object. It is preserving enough context through every layer that the final response can be justified.

ObserveCollect and normalize the signal.
CorrelateConnect detections, identities, locations and events.
AssessDetermine behavior, risk and threat context.
DecideEvaluate policy and generate a recommendation.
RespondExecute a governed action or escalate to an operator.
AttestPreserve evidence and delivery context.
