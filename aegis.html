AEGIS SECURITY PLATFORM / 02

Autonomous Security. Intelligent Response.

AEGIS is the central systems project in this portfolio: an AI-enabled security platform designed to perceive environments, establish context, assess risk, make decisions and orchestrate controlled response in real time.

01 / PERCEPTION

See Everything

Camera streams, sensor telemetry, event feeds and identity signals form a continuous perception layer. The system is designed to move from live media to structured events rather than treating video as passive footage.

02 / INTELLIGENCE

Understand Everything

Detection, identity intelligence, behavioral analysis, incident correlation and threat enrichment convert observations into security context that an operator can reason about.

03 / PREDICTION

Assess What Comes Next

Risk scoring and threat prediction create a forward-looking layer: not just what happened, but how severe the situation is, what evidence supports it and what should be considered next.

04 / AUTONOMY

Act Intelligently

Decision engines, playbooks, escalation and autonomous SOC services turn intelligence into controlled operational response while preserving policy and audit boundaries.

Identity Intelligence

The identity subsystem is designed as a first-class security capability: person identity records, UUID/external identifiers, face embeddings, model versions, quality scores, camera provenance, detection linkage, watchlist promotion, POI promotion, cross-camera matching, re-identification, risk/threat scoring, merge workflows and audit services.

Behavior & Threat Intelligence

Behavior engines provide a bridge between object detection and security meaning. Loitering, crowd behavior, tailgating and anomaly services produce behavior events and alerts; threat prediction and assessment then provide the risk context needed by decision intelligence.

Autonomous SOC

The response layer is being designed around playbooks, execution state, decision actions, escalation scheduling and controlled autonomy. The goal is not blind automation: it is a system that can explain why an action is recommended, execute within policy and leave an auditable operational trail.
