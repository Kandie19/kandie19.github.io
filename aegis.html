AEGIS SECURITY PLATFORM / 02

Autonomous Security. Intelligent Response.

AEGIS is an autonomous security and intelligence platform designed to perceive, understand, predict and respond to threats in real time.

PERCEPTION

See Everything

Camera ingestion, sensor telemetry, event streams, face signals and operational data enter a unified perception layer. RTSP/WebRTC streaming and computer-vision pipelines provide the real-time foundation.

INTELLIGENCE

Understand Everything

Identity intelligence, detection, behavioral analytics, threat scoring, incident correlation and decision intelligence transform raw events into contextual security knowledge.

AUTONOMY

Act Intelligently

Response orchestration, SOAR playbooks, autonomous SOC services, escalation logic and decision actions turn intelligence into controlled operational response.

IDENTITY

Face & Identity Intelligence

Identity services support person identities, face embeddings, watchlists, POI promotion, cross-camera matching, re-identification, scoring, audit trails and SOC linkage.
